Food Glucose Atlas / 食物血糖图谱

Photo credits, source pages, changes and license links are shown in each
illustrated food record and recorded in food-photos.js. Each photograph
retains its credited license, including the license of any adapted thumbnail.

Study sources, data terms, authors and links appear under Studies & data
sources and within each food record. Third-party photographs and datasets
retain their respective terms. The international GI research workbook
is loaded only on request in the reader's browser for personal
noncommercial research.
