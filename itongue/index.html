FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good to know.

What can I do with iTongue?+

You can add tongue and face images, complete adult cold/hot or child-focused questionnaires, review saved history, view practitioner recommendations, and manage your profile.

Does iTongue provide a medical diagnosis?+

No. Results and prompts are for wellness reference only and should not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

How much does the app cost?+

iTongue is currently listed as free on the U.S. App Store.

Which devices are supported?+

The current version requires iOS 17 or later on iPhone, or iPadOS 17 or later on iPad.

Where can I review privacy information?+

See the current disclosures on the iTongue App Store page and the linked privacy policy.
