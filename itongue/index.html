FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good to know.

What can I do with iTongue?+

You can upload a tongue image to receive your cold/hot tendency and TCM constitution information, add face records, complete adult cold/hot or child-focused questionnaires, review saved history, view practitioner recommendations, and manage your profile.

Does iTongue provide a medical diagnosis?+

No. Results and prompts are for wellness reference only and should not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

How much does the app cost?+

iTongue is currently listed as free on the U.S. App Store.
