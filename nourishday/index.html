ABOUT THE NOURISHDAY APP

Good to know.

What is NourishDay?＋

NourishDay: Food Calendar is a bilingual food diary and nutrition tracker from Jianna Consulting, LLC. It combines meal-photo logging, calorie and nutrient estimates, a nutrition calendar, and seasonal food ideas. Its Chinese name is 食养日历：NourishDay.

How does the photo food diary work?＋

Take a meal photo or choose an existing image, then confirm before it uploads for analysis. AI suggests foods and portions; nutrient estimates come from the reviewed food catalog. Correct the foods and amounts, then confirm the record you want to save. A photo cannot measure exact nutrition.

Can I track calories, protein and other nutrients?＋

Yes. Saved meals contribute to calorie, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber and other nutrient totals. Open a date in the nutrition calendar to review the meals behind that day’s estimates. You can also enter foods manually.

What does seasonal eating mean in the app?＋

NourishDay connects local weather and the 24 solar terms with food, tea and everyday wellness reading. Traditional seasonal guidance is educational context, not a measured nutritional effect or a treatment recommendation.

Where can I download NourishDay?＋

Download NourishDay: Food Calendar on the App Store. The app supports iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later, with a companion Apple Watch nutrition view. English and Simplified Chinese are included.

Is NourishDay free to use?＋

Yes. After sign-in, the ongoing free plan includes the five core tabs, up to three image analyses per day, and the latest seven calendar days of nutrition history. Optional Premium offers up to twenty analyses per day, full nutrition history and cross-device sync. Subscription prices and trial eligibility are shown in the app.

Is NourishDay medical advice?＋

No. Food recognition, portion estimates, nutrient totals and seasonal wellness content are for education and general wellness. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical conditions, allergies, pregnancy, medication or dietary restrictions.
